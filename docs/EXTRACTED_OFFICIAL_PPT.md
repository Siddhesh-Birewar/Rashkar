PROBLEM STATEMENT / IDEA
The Problem: 
India's 100M+ agricultural workers face lethal heat stress (280+
heatwave days in 2024, +87% vs 2023) causing 1,832 deaths 
(40-50% farmers), while PMFBY ( Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana) crop-insurance claims
take 45–60 days due to manual, district-level verification, causing 25–30% rejections and
₹ 1,500 Cr/year fraud. [Data: IMD 2024, NCRB 2024, MoA&FW 2024, ILO 2024].
Why Important?
86% of 12 crore PMFBY
farmers are <2 ha
smallholders – most climate-
vulnerable.
Heatwaves reduce
agricultural productivity by
30–40% (ILO 2024).
No integrated AI system exists
for farm-level heat-risk + plot-
level crop-loss.
Current Gaps:
❌ 86% of 12 crore PMFBY
farmers are < 2 hectares
smallholders – most climate-
vulnerable.
❌ Heatwaves reduce

30–40% (International Labour
Organization 2024).
❌ No integrated AI system

 RASHKAR - AI platform that
predicts personalized farm-

using multimodal AI.

## === PAGE 8 ===

Conclusion
OUR IDEA
RAHSKAR is an AI-powered platform that protects farmers before climate risk and supports them after crop loss.
It combines personalized heat-risk prediction with fast, plot-level PMFBY loss verification to make insurance assessment quicker,
fairer and more transparent.
WHY SHOULD WE BE SELECTED?
• Solves two critical problems together - Farmer heat safety + Crop-insurance delays.
• Built for real farmers - Farm-level predictions, smallholder plot verification and multilingual access.
• Reduces the biggest bottleneck: Time - targeting 45 days →  48 hours for crop-loss verification.
• Scalable impact - Cloud-based architecture designed to extend across crops, states and millions of farmers.
